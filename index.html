The JOE'S experience

A place for good food, good coffee and good company.

JOE'S is an artisan café and bakery in Blantyre, bringing together handmade breads, confectionery, fresh food, specialty treats and coffee in one welcoming space.

Everything is made in-house for a true artisanal experience — from everyday favourites to beautifully made custom cakes.
